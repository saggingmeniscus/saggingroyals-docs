# Uploading Sales Reports

The core function of Sagging Royalties is aggregating your sales data.

## Supported Formats

We support standard reports from:
*   Asterism
*   Ingram Lightning Source / Core Source
*   Amazon KDP
*   Draft2Digital
*   Snipcart
*   And more...

## How to Upload

1.  Navigate to **Sales > Upload Report**.
2.  Select the **Channel** (e.g., Ingram).
3.  Choose the **Format** you are uploading.
4.  Select the file from your computer.
5.  Click **Upload**.

The system will parse the file, match ISBNs to your catalog, and calculate royalties based on your formulas.

## Errors

If the system encounters ISBNs not in your catalog or unrecognized formats, it will flag these rows for review.
