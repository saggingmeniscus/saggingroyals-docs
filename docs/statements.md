# Royalty Statements

## Generating Statements

Statements are usually generated on a schedule (e.g., semi-annually).

1.  Navigate to **Royalties > Generate Statements**.
2.  Select the **Period** (e.g., Jan 1 2025 – Jun 30 2025).
3.  Click **Generate**.

## Reviewing Statements

You can review draft statements before they are published to authors. Check for:
*   Unassigned sales
*   Negative balances
*   Missing payment details

## Publishing

Once reviewed, you can "Publish" statements. This makes them visible to your authors in their portal.
